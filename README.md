# Power-BI-Sales-and-Profit-Dashboard
This Power BI dashboard provides a comprehensive analysis of Maven Cycles’ business performance across products, geographies, and customer demographics. It delivers data-driven insights to support better decisions in product strategy, regional targeting, and profit optimization.

The goal is to visualize **revenue growth**, **profit margins**, and **sales insights** across multiple dimensions using Power BI’s analytical capabilities.

---

## 🧩 Objectives
- Compare **Revenue, Cost, and Profit** by Product Category.
- Track **Monthly and Year-over-Year (YOY)** revenue variance.
- Explore **Quantity Sold** across **price ranges**, **countries**, and **age groups**.
- Identify **key influencers** that impact total revenue.
- Analyze **gender-based and age-based** sales distribution.

---

## 🗂️ Data Sources
- Maven Cycles Sample Dataset (from Maven Analytics)
- Data imported as `.csv` and transformed in **Power Query**
- Calculations built using **DAX** (Profit %, YOY Growth, Revenue Variance)

---

## ⚙️ Tools & Technologies

| Tool | Purpose |
|------|----------|
| **Power BI Desktop** | Data modeling, visualization, and storytelling |
| **DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)** | Measures and calculated columns |
| **Excel / CSV** | Source data |
| **Power Query** | ETL (data cleaning and transformations) |

---

## 📈 Dashboard Highlights
- **Revenue & Profit Overview** – Compare cost, revenue, and profit per product category.
- **Sales by Age & Gender** – Understand who buys the most and what they prefer.
- **Geographic Analysis** – Country-level sales, profit, and margin breakdown.
- **Revenue Trends** – Visualize monthly and yearly revenue variance.
- **Top Products & Categories** – Identify best-selling products and high-margin categories.
- **Key Influencers** – Discover which sub-categories impact total revenue the most.

---

## 💡 Key Insights
- 🚴 **Bikes** account for nearly **66% of total revenue** with the highest profitability.  
- 🎽 **Accessories** drive large revenue variance despite smaller volumes.  
- 👩‍🦰 **Adults (35–64)** form the largest customer segment.  
- 🌏 **United States and Australia** are the top-performing countries.  
- 📈 YOY revenue shows steady growth from 2016–2020 with moderate seasonality.
